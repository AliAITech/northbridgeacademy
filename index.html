About the Academy

Built inside a clinic that serves the community every day

NorthBridge Academy is an initiative of PurpleLife Psychology Clinic in Richmond Hill. We saw how many capable people are held back not by talent, but by the length and cost of traditional programs — so we designed something different: focused, practical training that respects your time and gets you working.
