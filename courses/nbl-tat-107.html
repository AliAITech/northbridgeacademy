Tattoo Fundamentals

About This Course

What this course covers

This introductory course familiarizes participants with tattoo-studio operations, artistic preparation, infection prevention, client consent, equipment awareness, stencil and design principles, aftercare communication, documentation, and professional ethics. Practice is limited to paper, synthetic practice media, and instructor-controlled demonstrations. The course does not authorize participants to tattoo human skin, perform invasive procedures independently, or represent themselves as licensed or fully qualified tattoo artists.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Describe the tattoo-service workflow, studio roles, and professional expectations.
  • Apply foundational infection-prevention, hygiene, barrier, and sharps-safety concepts in simulations.
  • Create simple, original tattoo designs and prepare stencils for synthetic practice media.
  • Recognize consent, age, privacy, cultural, copyright, and client-suitability considerations.
  • Communicate aftercare information and maintain basic records.
  • Develop a supervised apprenticeship and portfolio-development plan.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Artists exploring tattoo-studio careers.
  • Newcomers seeking an introduction to personal-service entrepreneurship.
  • Studio assistants seeking stronger hygiene and customer-service foundations.
  • Individuals considering a supervised tattoo apprenticeship.

Delivery & Assessment

How the course is delivered and assessed

Delivery and learning methods: Instructor-led presentations, guided discussion, demonstrations, case studies, individual and group exercises, role-play or simulations, applied assignments, structured feedback, and a final practical or integrative activity appropriate to the subject area.

Assessment: Safety and hygiene checks 25%; design and stencil portfolio 25%; simulated station setup and breakdown 25%; final synthetic-media project and presentation 25%.

Certificate requirements: Minimum 80% attendance, completion of required learning activities and assessments, and a minimum overall grade of 70%. The certificate identifies the course title and scheduled duration and does not represent a licence, regulated credential, diploma, or guarantee of employment.

Required materials and technology: Sketchbook, pencils, markers, protective equipment, instructor-approved synthetic practice media, and studio supplies provided or specified by the instructor.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1Tattoo Industry and Studio Roles

    History and current industry, artist and assistant roles, apprenticeship culture, professionalism, and realistic career expectations.

    Learning objectives: Describe studio roles; identify apprenticeship expectations; recognize professional standards.

  2. Session 2Legal, Public-Health, and Scope Awareness

    Consent, age verification, record keeping, public-health expectations, municipal considerations, insurance, and limits of this course.

    Learning objectives: Identify key compliance areas; recognize scope limits; explain why supervised progression is required.

  3. Session 3Infection Prevention Foundations

    Bloodborne pathogen awareness, hand hygiene, surface disinfection, single-use items, barriers, and contamination pathways.

    Learning objectives: Explain contamination risks; apply barrier concepts; follow hand-hygiene procedures.

  4. Session 4Sharps and Waste Safety Awareness

    Needle and sharps hazards, approved containers, exposure response, waste separation, and strict simulation-only handling.

    Learning objectives: Recognize sharps risks; describe safe disposal; outline exposure-response steps.

  5. Session 5Equipment Awareness and Station Organization

    Tattoo machine types, power supplies, cartridges, grips, inks, work surfaces, and safe simulated station layout.

    Learning objectives: Identify equipment; organize a clean workflow; distinguish clean and contaminated zones.

  6. Session 6Skin Awareness and Client Suitability

    Basic skin structure, common visible concerns, scars, sun damage, medications and health questions as referral considerations, and refusal of unsafe service.

    Learning objectives: Recognize visible concerns; identify referral needs; avoid medical assessment.

  7. Session 7Drawing Foundations for Tattoo Design

    Line, shape, contrast, composition, readability, scale, placement, and adapting art for tattoo applications.

    Learning objectives: Create readable designs; use contrast effectively; adapt artwork to scale.

  8. Session 8Originality, Copyright, and Cultural Respect

    Reference use, avoiding plagiarism, permissions, cultural symbols, respectful consultation, and maintaining an original portfolio.

    Learning objectives: Respect copyright; develop original work; recognize cultural sensitivity.

  9. Session 9Stencil Preparation and Placement Simulation

    Design transfer, stencil preparation, orientation, symmetry, body-contour awareness using mannequins or synthetic surfaces.

    Learning objectives: Prepare a clean stencil; position designs accurately; evaluate placement.

  10. Session 10Line Practice on Synthetic Media

    Hand stability, speed awareness, consistent line exercises, ergonomics, and instructor-controlled equipment or pen simulations.

    Learning objectives: Improve line consistency; use safe posture; evaluate practice results.

  11. Session 11Shading and Colour-Theory Foundations

    Value, gradients, packing concepts, complementary colour, skin-tone awareness, and paper or synthetic-media exercises.

    Learning objectives: Apply basic value and colour theory; create controlled gradients; plan colour palettes.

  12. Session 12Client Consultation and Informed Consent

    Understanding client ideas, managing expectations, confirming design and placement, consent documentation, and declining unsuitable requests.

    Learning objectives: Conduct a structured consultation; document consent; manage expectations ethically.

  13. Session 13Aftercare Communication

    General aftercare information, warning signs requiring medical attention, avoiding medical claims, and written instructions.

    Learning objectives: Explain general aftercare; identify referral warning signs; provide clear written information.

  14. Session 14Studio Customer Service and Business Basics

    Scheduling, deposits, cancellations, pricing awareness, portfolio presentation, social media boundaries, and complaint handling.

    Learning objectives: Explain studio policies; present work professionally; respond to concerns appropriately.

  15. Session 15Final Synthetic-Media Project and Apprenticeship Plan

    Design, stencil, simulated station setup, synthetic-media execution, breakdown, portfolio presentation, and supervised career pathway.

    Learning objectives: Integrate safe workflow; present a completed practice project; create an apprenticeship plan.

Scope & Limitations

Introductory only. No tattooing of human skin is included or authorized. Participants must comply with public health, municipal, insurance, apprenticeship, and studio requirements before providing services.
