About This Course

What this course covers

This practical course helps microbusiness owners, freelancers, and aspiring entrepreneurs use accessible artificial-intelligence tools to improve research, marketing, customer service, documentation, productivity, and workflow automation. Participants learn prompt design, quality control, privacy, copyright, bias, responsible use, and implementation planning. The course focuses on business application rather than software development.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Explain practical AI concepts relevant to small business.
  • Use generative AI to support research, writing, marketing, customer communication, and operations.
  • Create effective prompts and evaluate outputs for accuracy, relevance, bias, and risk.
  • Design simple human-supervised AI workflows using no-code tools.
  • Protect confidential information and apply responsible-use principles.
  • Build and present one practical AI implementation for a microbusiness.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Microbusiness owners, freelancers, and entrepreneurs.
  • Newcomers building small businesses in Canada.
  • Administrative, marketing, and customer-service professionals.
  • Non-technical learners seeking practical AI skills.

Delivery & Assessment

How the course is delivered and assessed

Delivery and learning methods: Instructor-led presentations, guided discussion, demonstrations, case studies, individual and group exercises, role-play or simulations, applied assignments, structured feedback, and a final practical or integrative activity appropriate to the subject area.

Assessment: Hands-on exercises 30%; prompt and output-quality portfolio 20%; workflow design 20%; final implementation project 30%.

Certificate requirements: Minimum 80% attendance, completion of required learning activities and assessments, and a minimum overall grade of 70%. The certificate identifies the course title and scheduled duration and does not represent a licence, regulated credential, diploma, or guarantee of employment.

Required materials and technology: Laptop, internet access, accounts for instructor-approved AI tools, spreadsheet and presentation software.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1AI Foundations for Microbusiness

    Generative AI, language models, image tools, automation, realistic capabilities, limitations, and business-use mapping.

    Learning objectives: Explain core concepts; distinguish tool types; identify suitable business use cases.

  2. Session 2Responsible Use, Privacy, and Risk

    Confidential data, hallucinations, bias, copyright, customer transparency, human oversight, and prohibited uses.

    Learning objectives: Identify AI risks; protect sensitive information; establish human-review rules.

  3. Session 3Prompt Engineering Fundamentals

    Context, role, task, constraints, examples, output format, iterative refinement, and reusable prompt templates.

    Learning objectives: Write structured prompts; improve weak outputs; create reusable templates.

  4. Session 4AI for Market and Customer Research

    Research questions, customer personas, competitor analysis, source verification, surveys, and insight synthesis.

    Learning objectives: Use AI to structure research; verify claims; summarize customer insights.

  5. Session 5AI for Brand Voice and Content Strategy

    Brand positioning, tone guides, content pillars, editorial calendars, and maintaining consistency.

    Learning objectives: Create a brand-voice guide; define content pillars; plan a content calendar.

  6. Session 6AI-Assisted Writing and Editing

    Emails, web copy, proposals, FAQs, social posts, translation support, editing, and accessibility.

    Learning objectives: Draft business content; edit for clarity; adapt content for audiences.

  7. Session 7AI for Visual and Presentation Content

    Image-generation principles, design briefs, slide outlines, visual consistency, permissions, and disclosure.

    Learning objectives: Write visual prompts; create design briefs; evaluate generated visuals responsibly.

  8. Session 8AI for Sales and Lead Management

    Lead qualification questions, outreach drafts, follow-up sequences, proposal personalization, CRM notes, and human approval.

    Learning objectives: Design a lead workflow; draft ethical outreach; improve follow-up consistency.

  9. Session 9AI for Customer Service

    FAQ libraries, chatbot concepts, response templates, sentiment cues, escalation rules, and service quality monitoring.

    Learning objectives: Build a response library; define escalation rules; design a customer-support flow.

  10. Session 10AI for Administration and Documentation

    Meeting summaries, action lists, forms, standard operating procedures, policies, and document quality control.

    Learning objectives: Create SOP drafts; convert notes into actions; apply document-review checklists.

  11. Session 11Data Analysis with AI Assistance

    Cleaning small datasets, asking analytical questions, interpreting tables, chart planning, and avoiding unsupported conclusions.

    Learning objectives: Formulate analysis questions; interpret basic results; validate conclusions.

  12. Session 12No-Code Automation Foundations

    Triggers, actions, conditions, approvals, integration examples, and mapping processes before automation.

    Learning objectives: Map a workflow; identify automation steps; include human approval points.

  13. Session 13Building a Simple AI Workflow

    Hands-on design using instructor-approved no-code tools for content, intake, follow-up, or reporting.

    Learning objectives: Configure a basic workflow; test inputs and outputs; document the process.

  14. Session 14AI Adoption, Change, and Measurement

    Use-case prioritization, cost, adoption, staff training, policies, KPIs, and continuous improvement.

    Learning objectives: Prioritize AI use cases; define success metrics; create an adoption checklist.

  15. Session 15Final Microbusiness AI Implementation

    Presentation of a practical AI workflow or operating system including problem, process, tools, controls, metrics, and next steps.

    Learning objectives: Integrate course skills; communicate business value; demonstrate responsible implementation.

Scope & Limitations

Tools and features may change. Participants remain responsible for human review, privacy, copyright, regulatory compliance, and professional advice. The course does not provide technical certification.
