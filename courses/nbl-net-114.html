About This Course

What this course covers

This course helps newcomers, job seekers, students, and early-stage entrepreneurs build professional relationships in Canada. Participants learn networking strategy, personal positioning, introductions, LinkedIn, informational interviews, events, follow-up, referrals, community engagement, boundaries, and relationship maintenance. The program emphasizes ethical, reciprocal networking rather than transactional solicitation.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Define clear networking goals and target communities.
  • Present skills, experience, and goals through a concise professional introduction.
  • Build and improve a credible LinkedIn profile.
  • Request and conduct informational conversations respectfully.
  • Follow up, maintain relationships, and ask for referrals appropriately.
  • Create and execute a 60-day networking plan.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Newcomers and refugees building Canadian professional networks.
  • Job seekers and career changers.
  • Students, freelancers, and emerging entrepreneurs.
  • Professionals seeking greater confidence at events and online.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1Networking Mindset and Strategy

    Purpose of networking, reciprocity, long-term relationships, hidden opportunities, ethics, and setting specific goals.

    Learning objectives: Define networking goals; distinguish relationship building from solicitation; identify target communities.

  2. Session 2Professional Story and Personal Positioning

    Transferable skills, achievements, career direction, value statement, and adapting one’s story to different audiences.

    Learning objectives: Identify key strengths; write a positioning statement; communicate career direction clearly.

  3. Session 3Introductions, Small Talk, and Confidence

    Opening conversations, asking good questions, listening, exiting politely, managing anxiety, and cultural differences.

    Learning objectives: Deliver a professional introduction; sustain a conversation; close interactions respectfully.

  4. Session 4LinkedIn Profile and Digital Credibility

    Headline, summary, experience, skills, profile image, portfolio evidence, privacy, and consistent online presence.

    Learning objectives: Improve profile sections; present evidence of skills; manage digital professionalism.

  5. Session 5Finding and Approaching the Right People

    Alumni, associations, community groups, employers, events, online communities, warm introductions, and outreach research.

    Learning objectives: Build a target list; identify warm paths; personalize outreach.

  6. Session 6Informational Interviews

    Request messages, preparation, questions, time management, gratitude, boundaries, and converting insight into action.

    Learning objectives: Request an informational interview; conduct a structured conversation; document insights.

  7. Session 7Events, Associations, and Community Engagement

    Preparing for events, conversation goals, volunteering, professional associations, job fairs, and community contribution.

    Learning objectives: Plan event participation; engage with associations; use volunteering strategically.

  8. Session 8Follow-Up, Referrals, and Relationship Maintenance

    Follow-up timing, useful touchpoints, giving value, referral requests, tracking contacts, and avoiding over-contact.

    Learning objectives: Write effective follow-up; request referrals appropriately; maintain a contact system.

  9. Session 9Networking Simulation and Feedback

    Rotating role-play of events, online outreach, informational interviews, and referral conversations with peer feedback.

    Learning objectives: Demonstrate networking skills; adapt communication; use feedback to improve.

  10. Session 10Final 60-Day Networking Plan

    Participants develop targets, weekly actions, events, outreach messages, follow-up schedule, measures, and accountability.

    Learning objectives: Create a realistic plan; define activity metrics; commit to consistent action.

Scope & Limitations

The course develops relationship-building skills and does not guarantee referrals, interviews, contracts, or employment.
