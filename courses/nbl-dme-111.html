About This Course

What this course covers

This course provides practical foundations in digital marketing for small businesses, freelancers, community organizations, and entry-level marketing roles. Participants learn audience research, positioning, content strategy, social media, websites, search visibility, email, paid advertising awareness, campaign planning, analytics, privacy, and optimization. The course culminates in a complete, measurable campaign plan.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Define target audiences, customer journeys, and clear marketing objectives.
  • Develop positioning, messaging, content pillars, and a practical publishing calendar.
  • Use social media, websites, local search, email, and paid-media concepts appropriately.
  • Create campaign assets and calls to action aligned with a consistent brand.
  • Measure reach, engagement, leads, conversion, and campaign efficiency.
  • Develop and present an integrated digital marketing campaign.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Small-business owners and freelancers.
  • Newcomers seeking entry-level digital-marketing skills.
  • Community and nonprofit staff responsible for outreach.
  • Job seekers building a practical marketing portfolio.

Delivery & Assessment

How the course is delivered and assessed

Delivery and learning methods: Instructor-led presentations, guided discussion, demonstrations, case studies, individual and group exercises, role-play or simulations, applied assignments, structured feedback, and a final practical or integrative activity appropriate to the subject area.

Assessment: Channel and audience exercises 20%; content portfolio 25%; analytics assignments 20%; final campaign plan and presentation 35%.

Certificate requirements: Minimum 80% attendance, completion of required learning activities and assessments, and a minimum overall grade of 70%. The certificate identifies the course title and scheduled duration and does not represent a licence, regulated credential, diploma, or guarantee of employment.

Required materials and technology: Laptop, internet access, spreadsheet and presentation software, and access to instructor-approved social and design tools.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1Digital Marketing Strategy

    Marketing objectives, audience, funnel, customer journey, owned/earned/paid media, and aligning marketing with business goals.

    Learning objectives: Set measurable objectives; map a customer journey; distinguish channel types.

  2. Session 2Audience Research and Personas

    Demographics, behaviours, needs, interviews, social listening, search intent, and evidence-based personas.

    Learning objectives: Research an audience; build a persona; identify pain points and motivations.

  3. Session 3Positioning, Messaging, and Offers

    Value proposition, competitive differentiation, trust, offer design, claims, calls to action, and message testing.

    Learning objectives: Write positioning statements; create a clear offer; develop calls to action.

  4. Session 4Brand Voice and Content Pillars

    Tone, language, visual consistency, content categories, educational versus promotional balance, and governance.

    Learning objectives: Define brand voice; select content pillars; maintain consistency.

  5. Session 5Content Planning and Production

    Editorial calendars, briefs, captions, short video, images, repurposing, accessibility, and approval workflows.

    Learning objectives: Build a content calendar; write creative briefs; repurpose content efficiently.

  6. Session 6Social Media Channel Strategy

    Platform selection, profiles, community engagement, scheduling, moderation, organic growth, and channel fit.

    Learning objectives: Select appropriate platforms; optimize profiles; plan engagement routines.

  7. Session 7Short-Form Video and Visual Storytelling

    Hooks, scripts, shot lists, demonstrations, testimonials, editing workflow, captions, and ethical storytelling.

    Learning objectives: Create a short video plan; write a script; apply accessible storytelling.

  8. Session 8Website and Landing-Page Foundations

    Page structure, headlines, benefits, social proof, forms, calls to action, mobile usability, and conversion principles.

    Learning objectives: Outline a landing page; write conversion-focused sections; assess usability.

  9. Session 9Search Engine and Local Visibility Basics

    Keywords, search intent, on-page basics, local profiles, reviews, citations, and content for discoverability.

    Learning objectives: Conduct basic keyword research; improve local visibility; plan search-focused content.

  10. Session 10Email Marketing Foundations

    Permission, list building, welcome sequences, newsletters, segmentation, subject lines, and performance measures.

    Learning objectives: Design an ethical list-building approach; draft an email sequence; measure results.

  11. Session 11Paid Advertising Awareness

    Campaign objectives, audiences, creative, budgets, bidding concepts, landing pages, and avoiding unverified claims.

    Learning objectives: Explain paid-ad structures; draft an ad concept; identify budget and risk controls.

  12. Session 12Lead Generation and Conversion

    Lead magnets, forms, consultations, follow-up, CRM awareness, conversion barriers, and sales-marketing alignment.

    Learning objectives: Design a lead funnel; improve follow-up; identify conversion barriers.

  13. Session 13Analytics and Measurement

    Reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, leads, conversion, cost metrics, dashboards, and interpretation.

    Learning objectives: Select KPIs; calculate basic rates; interpret campaign performance.

  14. Session 14Optimization, Privacy, and Ethics

    A/B testing, iteration, cookies and consent awareness, privacy, influencer disclosure, accessibility, and ethical claims.

    Learning objectives: Design a simple test; identify privacy concerns; apply ethical marketing standards.

  15. Session 15Final Integrated Campaign Plan

    Presentation of objectives, audience, message, channels, calendar, assets, budget, KPIs, risks, and optimization plan.

    Learning objectives: Integrate course skills; justify channel choices; present a measurable campaign.

Scope & Limitations

Platform features, advertising rules, and privacy requirements may change. The course does not guarantee leads, sales, platform approval, or employment.
