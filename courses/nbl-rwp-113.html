Remote Work Productivity

Program Code: NBL-RWP-113

About This Course

What this course covers

This course prepares participants to work effectively in remote and hybrid environments. It covers workspace setup, digital communication, time and task management, virtual meetings, collaboration tools, documentation, cybersecurity awareness, performance visibility, boundaries, wellbeing, and job readiness. Participants build a practical personal remote-work system.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Set up an organized, safe, and professional remote-work environment.
  • Use digital communication and collaboration tools appropriately.
  • Plan tasks, priorities, deadlines, and focus time effectively.
  • Participate in virtual meetings and maintain clear documentation.
  • Apply basic cybersecurity, privacy, and information-handling practices.
  • Create a sustainable remote-work productivity and communication plan.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Job seekers targeting remote or hybrid roles.
  • Newcomers adapting to Canadian digital workplaces.
  • Employees seeking stronger remote-work habits.
  • Freelancers and small-business operators working virtually.

Delivery & Assessment

How the course is delivered and assessed

Delivery and learning methods: Instructor-led presentations, guided discussion, demonstrations, case studies, individual and group exercises, role-play or simulations, applied assignments, structured feedback, and a final practical or integrative activity appropriate to the subject area.

Assessment: Participation and tool exercises 25%; communication and meeting tasks 25%; productivity-system assignment 20%; final remote-work plan and simulation 30%.

Certificate requirements: Minimum 80% attendance, completion of required learning activities and assessments, and a minimum overall grade of 70%. The certificate identifies the course title and scheduled duration and does not represent a licence, regulated credential, diploma, or guarantee of employment.

Required materials and technology: Laptop or desktop computer, stable internet, headset, webcam where available, calendar, word-processing, and collaboration applications.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1Remote and Hybrid Work Foundations

    Work models, employer expectations, self-management, communication visibility, common challenges, and role suitability.

    Learning objectives: Describe remote-work expectations; identify personal challenges; assess role readiness.

  2. Session 2Workspace, Ergonomics, and Professional Presence

    Workspace setup, posture, lighting, sound, background, equipment, accessibility, and household boundaries.

    Learning objectives: Improve workspace safety; create a professional setup; identify equipment needs.

  3. Session 3Time, Energy, and Priority Management

    Calendars, task lists, priorities, time blocking, focus cycles, interruptions, and realistic workload planning.

    Learning objectives: Prioritize work; build a weekly plan; reduce common distractions.

  4. Session 4Digital Communication and Response Standards

    Email, chat, status updates, tone, response times, asynchronous communication, and escalation.

    Learning objectives: Choose the right channel; write concise updates; set response expectations.

  5. Session 5Virtual Meetings and Facilitation

    Agendas, preparation, participation, screen sharing, note-taking, action items, accessibility, and meeting reduction.

    Learning objectives: Participate professionally; document decisions; facilitate a short meeting.

  6. Session 6Collaboration and Document Management

    Shared files, naming conventions, version control, comments, permissions, task boards, and knowledge bases.

    Learning objectives: Organize shared documents; manage versions; collaborate transparently.

  7. Session 7Cybersecurity and Privacy Awareness

    Passwords, multifactor authentication, phishing, device updates, secure networks, confidential information, and incident reporting.

    Learning objectives: Apply basic security practices; recognize phishing; protect confidential data.

  8. Session 8Performance, Accountability, and Boundaries

    Outcome-based work, progress reporting, availability, workload conversations, breaks, disconnection, and burnout prevention.

    Learning objectives: Make work visible; communicate workload; create healthy boundaries.

  9. Session 9Remote Job Readiness and Simulation

    Remote-job search signals, interviews, technology checks, home-office questions, onboarding, and simulated workday tasks.

    Learning objectives: Prepare for remote interviews; demonstrate digital professionalism; manage simulated tasks.

  10. Session 10Final Personal Remote-Work Operating System

    Participants present a complete system for workspace, schedule, tools, communication, meetings, security, and wellbeing.

    Learning objectives: Integrate course practices; present a sustainable system; define improvement metrics.

Scope & Limitations

The course develops general skills and does not guarantee remote employment or certify proficiency in a specific software platform.
