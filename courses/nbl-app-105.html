Appliance Installation Basics

Program Code: NBL-APP-105
Duration30 scheduled hours
Sessions15 sessions × 2 hours (1 hour 45 minutes of instruction and practice, plus a 15-minute break)
Delivery ModeIn-Person
Course TypeShort Course / Practical
CredentialNorthBridge Academy Certificate of Completion

About This Course

What this course covers

This hands-on introductory course develops foundational skills for assisting with the installation of common household appliances. Participants learn worksite assessment, tool use, manufacturer instructions, basic electrical and plumbing awareness, levelling and securing appliances, testing, documentation, customer communication, and safe troubleshooting. Training is limited to non-regulated introductory tasks and simulations; electrical, gas, refrigeration, or plumbing work requiring a licensed trade must be referred to appropriately qualified professionals.

Learning Objectives

What you will learn

  • Identify common household appliances, installation requirements, and manufacturer documentation.
  • Use basic hand tools and measuring equipment safely.
  • Prepare installation areas and complete non-regulated connection and positioning tasks in simulations.
  • Recognize hazards and work that requires a licensed electrician, gas technician, plumber, or refrigeration professional.
  • Complete testing, customer handover, documentation, and basic service communication.
  • Develop an employment or supervised self-employment action plan.

Target Audience

Who this course is for

  • Newcomers interested in home-service occupations.
  • Entry-level helpers working with appliance installers or service companies.
  • Individuals exploring supervised self-employment opportunities.
  • Maintenance and property-service workers seeking foundational awareness.

Delivery & Assessment

How the course is delivered and assessed

Delivery and learning methods: Instructor-led presentations, guided discussion, demonstrations, case studies, individual and group exercises, role-play or simulations, applied assignments, structured feedback, and a final practical or integrative activity appropriate to the subject area.

Assessment: Safety and knowledge checks 20%; tool-use demonstrations 20%; installation simulations 35%; documentation and customer handover 10%; final practical project 15%.

Certificate requirements: Minimum 80% attendance, completion of required learning activities and assessments, and a minimum overall grade of 70%. The certificate identifies the course title and scheduled duration and does not represent a licence, regulated credential, diploma, or guarantee of employment.

Required materials and technology: Work clothing, safety footwear, safety glasses, gloves, tape measure, basic hand-tool kit, and manufacturer manuals supplied or approved by the instructor.

Course Outline

Session by session

  1. Session 1Industry and Appliance Overview

    Roles in appliance delivery, installation, service, and repair; major appliance categories; customer and employer expectations.

    Learning objectives: Identify appliance categories; describe entry-level roles; recognize professional expectations.

  2. Session 2Workplace Safety and Hazard Recognition

    PPE, lifting hazards, sharp edges, electricity, water, gas, confined spaces, and stop-work decisions.

    Learning objectives: Select basic PPE; identify hazards; recognize when work must stop or be referred.

  3. Session 3Tools, Fasteners, and Measuring

    Hand tools, drivers, levels, measuring, anchors, fasteners, tool inspection, and storage.

    Learning objectives: Choose appropriate tools; measure accurately; maintain tools safely.

  4. Session 4Reading Manufacturer Instructions

    Model identification, specifications, clearances, installation diagrams, warranties, and documenting compliance.

    Learning objectives: Interpret manuals; verify clearances; document installation requirements.

  5. Session 5Site Assessment and Customer Confirmation

    Access routes, floor protection, utilities, cabinet openings, appliance condition, and confirming scope with customers.

    Learning objectives: Conduct a site check; identify constraints; confirm scope and exclusions.

  6. Session 6Safe Handling, Moving, and Positioning

    Team lifting, dollies, straps, floor protection, unpacking, inspection, and damage prevention.

    Learning objectives: Plan safe movement; use handling aids; prevent property and product damage.

  7. Session 7Electrical Awareness and Referral Boundaries

    Voltage labels, grounded outlets, cord inspection, disconnect awareness, and tasks reserved for licensed electricians.

    Learning objectives: Recognize basic electrical requirements; identify unsafe conditions; refer regulated work.

  8. Session 8Water Connection Awareness

    Supply hoses, shut-off valves, drainage, leak prevention, and plumbing referral boundaries.

    Learning objectives: Identify water connections; apply leak-prevention checks; refer plumbing deficiencies.

  9. Session 9Dishwasher Installation Simulation

    Opening measurements, levelling, securing, drain routing, water and power awareness, and test procedures in a training environment.

    Learning objectives: Sequence a dishwasher installation; level and secure equipment; complete test checks.

  10. Session 10Laundry Appliance Installation

    Washer hoses, drain setup, levelling, dryer venting awareness, stacking requirements, and customer handover.

    Learning objectives: Set up laundry appliances; recognize venting risks; perform basic operational checks.

  11. Session 11Refrigerator and Ice-Maker Awareness

    Clearances, levelling, door alignment, water-line awareness, transportation timing, and customer instructions.

    Learning objectives: Position and level refrigerators; identify water-line referral needs; explain startup precautions.

  12. Session 12Range, Cooktop, and Gas Safety Boundaries

    Anti-tip devices, clearances, electrical and gas hazards, and strict referral requirements for regulated connections.

    Learning objectives: Recognize anti-tip requirements; identify regulated tasks; refuse unsafe or unauthorized work.

  13. Session 13Built-In and Specialty Appliance Awareness

    Microwaves, wall ovens, range hoods, mounting considerations, cabinetry, ventilation, and licensed work interfaces.

    Learning objectives: Identify specialty requirements; assess mounting risks; coordinate with qualified trades.

  14. Session 14Testing, Troubleshooting, and Quality Control

    Visual inspection, levelling, leak checks, vibration, error codes, documentation, and distinguishing installation from repair issues.

    Learning objectives: Perform basic quality checks; isolate common setup issues; refer repair faults.

  15. Session 15Final Installation Project and Employment Plan

    Integrated installation simulation, safety review, customer handover, invoice/work-order basics, and next-step employment planning.

    Learning objectives: Demonstrate a safe installation workflow; complete handover documentation; create a career action plan.

Scope & Limitations

Introductory and non-licensing. The course does not authorize regulated electrical, gas, refrigeration, or plumbing work and does not guarantee trade certification or employment.

In practice, the course covers positioning appliances and plugging them into existing outlets only — no electrical, plumbing, or gas connections are performed.
